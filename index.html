ただいま

We named it after the word you say when you come home.

Tadaima is the small phrase called out at the door, the one that means “I’m home.” It is the feeling we build the whole cafe around: Japanese home cooking, made simply and served with care. Soft milk bread, tea roasted slowly, matcha whisked by hand. Nothing fussy, just the things that make a day feel looked after.

The Room

A small room, warm wood, and a long pause in the day.

The Craft

Whisked by hand,
built to order.

Every matcha is sifted and whisked to a fine foam, then built over ice and finished to order. Sandos are layered on pillowy milk bread, strawberry and cream, kiwi and chocolate, crisp katsu. The hojicha is slow-roasted until it turns toasty and amber.

It is a short menu on purpose. We would rather make a few things properly than a great many things in a hurry.
