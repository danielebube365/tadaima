The Culture

Food that
feels like home.

Tadaima is built on Japanese home cooking, the simple, comforting things made in a family kitchen. Soft milk bread, slow-roasted tea, matcha whisked by hand. Nothing fussy, just the flavors that feel like being looked after.

We wanted a corner of San Francisco that felt that way the moment you walked in, where the regulars are on a first-name basis and the green cup is a small daily ritual.

ただいま tadaima  /  what you say when you walk through the door. “I’m home.”
